Privacy policy for Archibald Nest

Archibald Nest is a private tool I built to run my own Google Nest thermostat. It isn’t offered to anyone else.

What it reads. Through Google’s Smart Device Management API, it reads my thermostat’s temperature, humidity, mode and set temperatures, and the home and room names Google shares with it.

What it does with that. It shows the readings to me in my own app and changes the thermostat when I ask it to. The data stays on my own computer.

Who else sees it. Nobody. It isn’t sold, shared or used for advertising.

Removing access. Access can be removed at any time under Third-party connections in your Google Account.

Questions. Email jack@jackraney.com.
