Putting

Overall putting includes every putt. The distance tables below use first putts only, because later-putt positions are less reliable.

GHIN checked 2026-10-30. On-course analysis: 5 rounds / 81 holes, 2026-04-15 to 2026-06-04.

How to read these pages

Three records: GHIN supplies posted scores and the official Handicap Index; Arccos supplies sensor shots for a local scratch comparison; TrackMan supplies practice measurements.

Strokes gained (SG): +1 means one stroke better than the scratch model; −1 means one worse. Summary rates are per 18 holes unless labeled per shot or actual round. Scratch means a 0.0 handicap.

Median: the middle result. Carry: flight to landing. Total distance: includes roll. Dispersion: how widely shots scatter. Expand the explanations beside a chart for its exact definition.

Evidence: samples are labeled, small groups are flagged, and uncertainty ranges do not account for every model assumption. A daily rebuild does not mean the underlying rounds are recent.

Focus area: keep measuring

No distance band has both enough first putts and a loss range entirely below zero. The lowest estimate is not yet a proven weakness.

Strokes gained
Strokes gained. Expected strokes before the shot, minus expected strokes afterward, minus the shot and any penalties. Positive is better than the scratch model; negative is worse. −2.0 per 18 holes means about two strokes lost. Category totals include all shots; the putting distance table covers first putts only.
The ranges
How to read the uncertainty ranges. These approximate 95% intervals describe sampling uncertainty under the model assumptions. They are not a 95% probability that a fixed true value lies inside the displayed band. A range touching or crossing zero does not clearly distinguish the result from scratch. Just five rounds, correlated shots and the calibrated baseline limit certainty beyond what these ranges capture.

Make rate
First-putt make rate. The percentage of first putts holed within each distance band. Later putts are excluded from this table. The comparison is a broad scratch reference, and the distances and sample mix may differ. Differences are percentage points: 33% versus 48% is 15 points lower.
Too few to read
"Too few to read". A distance bucket with fewer than 8 shots behind it is greyed out and never chosen as a focus area. Three good chips in a row is not evidence. Under 3 shots it is not published at all.

Three-putts

8 three-putt holes. On them my first putt averaged 39 ft, against 19 ft on holes I two-putted. Long first putts are what precedes them.

Arccos does not record where the second putt was played from, so I cannot say whether these were poor lags or missed short ones. Only the first putt is measured.

Three-putts
Three-putts. Three or more putts on one hole. What matters is what preceded them: the length of the first putt. The data records where each putt started but not how far the second one was left, so I can say long first putts come before three-putts, and not whether the lag or the short one was the miss.
