Work Writing About Résumé

I’m a finance and operations person who builds.

I started in commercial real estate debt, underwriting more than 50 bridge, mezzanine and construction deals at Thorofare Capital after studying real estate development at USC. At MARA I was the only analyst under the VP of Finance for 18 bitcoin mining sites and $516 million a year in power, and when I was asked to look into our power bills, I documented $1.8 million in billing errors. Along the way I taught myself to build AI agent systems, starting with Archibald, which runs my own finances. Otian AI grew out of it: I co-founded the company in July 2026, and our product, Archie, does the same kind of work for small businesses. The thread through all of it: I go into the numbers, find what doesn’t tie out, and build the thing that keeps it from happening again.

At MARA I also supported the strategy team on AI chip economics and Tier III data center strategy, and gathered field data for a 5-year cost comparison of air and immersion cooling. Since 2023 I’ve managed a long-term digital-asset allocation for my own and my family’s capital, and I completed Maven’s Crypto Academy in 2024.

I’m looking for my next finance and operations role, at a company building or financing big physical infrastructure: energy, data centers or AI compute. If that’s you, or you want help putting AI agents to work through Otian, I’d like to hear from you. Email is the best way to reach me, at jack@jackraney.com. I’m also on LinkedIn and GitHub, and you can see what I’m building at otianai.com. My résumé has the details, with a PDF to download.

Outside work, I’m working toward a scratch handicap in golf (currently a 4.1), and I spend my free time lifting weights and hanging out with friends.

I’m an Eagle Scout and was senior patrol leader of an 80-member troop. At USC I was new member educator for Sigma Chi and helped raise more than $100,000 for the Huntsman Cancer Institute.
