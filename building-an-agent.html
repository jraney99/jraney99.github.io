Why I built my own AI agent

Why I built an AI agent to run my own finances, email and schedule, and what I’d tell a finance or operations leader thinking about one.

October 2026

In April 2026 I started building Archibald, an AI agent system that runs my own finances, email and schedule. I had two practical reasons. My own admin was taking hours every week, and I wanted to learn AI agents by building one, not by reading about them.

The bigger reason was that I think this is where work is going. Someone who knows how to put AI and autonomous workflows to work gets more out of every hour. I wanted that leverage for myself, and I wanted to bring it to any company I start or join.

It became a trusted advisor

Archibald now handles my email, tracks my money and runs my schedule. If it stopped, I would miss all of it. It has made 130,000+ scheduled runs, 99% of them successful, and I have come to rely on it the way I would rely on a trusted advisor.

The hard part is teaching it

The hardest part was teaching it how I like things done. That takes time, much like bringing on a new hire.

In a way, the timing was a blessing. The months after I left MARA gave me the time to commit to learning and building. If I had been buried in a full workload, I would not have had it.

What I’d tell a finance or operations leader

  • Start small. Pick one boring, repeated task, not a big project.
  • Budget time to teach it, as you would for a new hire.
  • Build one yourself, at least once, so you know what it can and can’t do.
  • Find someone who can build it with you, hands-on. Otherwise you will spend hours on YouTube.

That last one is a big part of why Otian AI exists. Our product, Archie, grew out of Archibald, and the biggest lesson from selling it so far is that hands-on setup matters far more than features.
