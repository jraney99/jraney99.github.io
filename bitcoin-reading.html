A bitcoin reading list

Where to start if you want to understand bitcoin properly: primary sources, free courses and books.

October 2026

The list runs from primary sources to free courses to books. Many of the books argue a case for bitcoin, so read them knowing that. Case Bitcoin, listed below, collects the common criticisms, though it answers them from a pro-bitcoin view.

Start with the source

  • The Bitcoin whitepaper Satoshi Nakamoto, 2008
    The nine-page paper that described the system.
  • The Bitcoin whitepaper, simplified Coinbase Learn
    A section-by-section walkthrough of the whitepaper for readers without a computer science background.
  • Bitcoin.org
    A community-run site with beginner guides, FAQs and links to wallets.
  • Satoshi Nakamoto Institute
    An archive of primary sources on bitcoin, cryptography and monetary history.
  • The Complete Satoshi
    Every known email, forum post and code comment from bitcoin’s creator, in order.
  • Inventing Bitcoin Yan Pritzker
    A short, clear explanation of how bitcoin works and which problems each piece solves.
  • Case Bitcoin
    Common criticisms of bitcoin with data and responses, organized by question.
  • Bitcoin Obituaries 99Bitcoins
    A running record of every time a major publication declared bitcoin dead.

Free courses

  • Learn Me A Bitcoin Greg Walker
    How bitcoin works from beginner to technical, with diagrams.
  • Bitcoin for Everybody Saylor Academy
    A free, self-paced course on bitcoin’s history, money and how to use it.
  • Khan Academy: Bitcoin
    Introductory videos that place bitcoin within money and banking.
  • River Learn
    Plain explainers and a glossary from a bitcoin brokerage.
  • My First Bitcoin
    An open-source education project with a free curriculum used in classrooms around the world.

Books

  • The Bitcoin Standard Saifedean Ammous
    A history of money from shells to gold to central banking, arguing that bitcoin is the next step.
  • Layered Money Nik Bhatia
    How money is built in layers, from gold to central bank reserves to bank deposits, and where bitcoin could fit.
  • Broken Money Lyn Alden
    How payment technology has shaped the monetary system, and why today’s system strains.
  • Digital Gold Nathaniel Popper
    A reported history of bitcoin’s early years and the people who built it.
  • The Blocksize War Jonathan Bier
    The 2015 to 2017 fight over how bitcoin should scale, told from the side favoring small blocks, which won.
  • The Book of Satoshi Phil Champagne
    Satoshi Nakamoto’s writings arranged by topic, with context.
  • 21 Lessons Gigi
    Short essays on what studying bitcoin teaches about money, economics and technology.
  • The Little Bitcoin Book Bitcoin Collective
    A short introduction for general readers.
  • Why Buy Bitcoin Andy Edstrom
    A financial adviser’s case for bitcoin as a long-term holding, including risk and volatility.
  • The Bullish Case for Bitcoin Vijay Boyapati
    An essay comparing bitcoin with gold and other assets. Later expanded into a book.
  • Bitcoin Is Venice Allen Farrington and Sacha Meyers
    Essays on capital, finance and civilization, drawing parallels between bitcoin and Renaissance Venice.
  • Softwar Jason Lowery
    An MIT thesis arguing that proof of work could matter for national security, not just money.
  • Bitcoin One Million Fred Krueger and Ben Sigman
    A speculative story of a world where bitcoin reaches $1 million. Read it as a scenario, not a forecast.

Adapted from the resource lists I wrote for Kingdom Bitcoin (February 2026). Several of these books argue a case; none of this is investment advice. Some terms are defined in my bitcoin glossary.
