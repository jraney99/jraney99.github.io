Work Writing About Résumé

I find what’s wrong in the numbers behind big operations, and I build the systems that fix it.

At MARA, I was the only analyst under the VP of Finance for 18 bitcoin mining sites: 1.8 GW of load and $516 million a year in power. When I was asked to look into our power bills, I traced $1.8 million in billing errors back to their source. I also helped model what it would take to convert mining sites to AI and HPC. Before that, I underwrote more than 50 commercial real estate loans at Thorofare Capital, across bridge, mezzanine and construction deals. Since then, I’ve built Archibald, an AI agent system that runs my own finances, email and schedule. It became the starting point for Otian AI, the company I co-founded to bring the same kind of agents to small businesses.
