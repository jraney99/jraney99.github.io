$10 a day in bitcoin, since 2017

What buying $10 of bitcoin every day from January 1, 2017 turned into, and what it felt like along the way.

October 2026

The plan is as simple as it gets: buy $10 of bitcoin every day, whatever the price. No timing and no selling. From January 1, 2017 through October 25, 2026, that is 3,585 purchases and $35,850 put in.

It ended with about 3.79 bitcoin, worth about $303,000 at October 25’s close of $80,053. That is 8.5 times the money that went in, at an average cost of about $9,460 per coin.

The part the total hides

  • 2018. At the end of 2017 the account held $3,650 of purchases worth $25,000. Twelve months later it held $7,300 of purchases worth $8,700. From its December 2017 peak to its December 2018 low it lost 79% of its value, and at the bottom it was worth $7,331 against $7,140 put in. It did not get back to the 2017 peak until July 2020.
  • 2022. The account peaked at $227,000 in November 2021 and fell 76%, to $55,000, by November 2022. It did not recover until February 2024.
  • Today. The all-time high was $466,000 on October 6, 2025. As of October 25, 2026, the account is 35% below it.

Only 23 of the 3,585 days ended with the account worth less than what had gone in, and all of them came in the first three months of 2017.

Why it worked

A fixed dollar amount buys more bitcoin when the price is low. The $10 a day put in during 2018 and 2022 bought several times more coin than the same $10 at the 2021 peak. The plan never had to get the timing right. It only had to keep going through two drops of more than 75%.

What this doesn’t tell you

  • Hindsight. 2017 is a start date chosen after the fact. Putting the whole $35,850 in on day one would be worth about $2.9 million today, but that meant buying everything at $993 and holding through both crashes.
  • Costs. It ignores fees, which can be steep on $10 purchases, and taxes.
  • The future. Past returns say nothing about what comes next. Bitcoin fell more than 75% twice in this window alone.

Why I do it

I’ve bought $10 of bitcoin a day for about the past year, for the reason this page shows. Over a long enough horizon, bitcoin has been one of the best investments anyone could have made. The same $10 a day put into the S&P 500 over the same dates, with dividends reinvested, would be worth about $78,800, or 2.2 times the money put in, against 8.5 times for bitcoin. Owning it through the drops is the hard part, and buying the same amount every day takes the decision out of it.

The math is simple. The hard part was 2018 and 2022, when the account lost three quarters of its value and the plan was to keep buying anyway.

Method: Coinbase BTC-USD daily closing prices (UTC); $10 bought at each close from January 1, 2017 to October 25, 2026; no fees or taxes. Recalculated daily. S&P 500 comparison: SPY dividend-adjusted daily closes from Yahoo Finance, with weekend and holiday purchases at the prior close. I own bitcoin. Nothing here is investment advice.
