Raw score and adjusted score
Raw score and adjusted score. GHIN caps every hole at net double bogey before it works out a handicap, so a blow-up hole counts for less than it cost me. The scorecard shows the raw score, what I actually made. The round total beside the course name is the adjusted one GHIN posted, which is why the two can differ.
Differential
Differential. One round’s score, adjusted for course difficulty, so a 79 on a hard course can beat a 76 on an easy one. A differential at or under my index means that round beat my handicap.
What a Handicap Index is
What a Handicap Index is. Not an average. Each round becomes a differential, which is the score adjusted for how hard that course plays. The index is the average of the best 8 of the last 20 differentials, so it describes my better days, not my typical ones. That is why my index is lower than my scoring average, and why both are true at once.
