What matters off the tee

I hit 26 of 63 fairways (41%). That number barely matters: in Shot Scope’s data, fairway percentage is almost flat from scratch to a 20 handicap. What separates handicaps off the tee is the disaster rate, penalty balls and the spots you cannot advance from. That is where my strokes go: the penalties alone cost about 2.4 strokes a round when they come from the tee.
