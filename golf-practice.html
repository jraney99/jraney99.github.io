Practice, measured

TrackMan practice sessions: speeds, carries, gapping and dispersion per club.

OverviewDrivingApproachShort gamePuttingRoundsPractice

This page is waiting on its data. I practice on a TrackMan simulator, and each session’s shots, club speed, ball speed, smash factor, carry and dispersion per club, will land here: a true gapping chart from measured carries, not guesses, and the spread of each club mapped the same way the approach page maps my on-course misses.

The point of putting practice data next to play data is the loop: if four weeks of wedge work do not move my proximity from 75–125 yards on the course, the practice was the wrong practice. The site will show both sides.

5 Arccos rounds, Apr to Jun 2026, 81 holes; strokes gained vs a scratch baseline calibrated to Arccos. I wear the sensors every round now, so this fills in as I play. Updated automatically.
